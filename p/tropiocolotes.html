About

20 supplied samples (CN10xxx, 13-19 sequences per locus, none yet in GenBank under those IDs) were put in context with a GenBank text-query snapshot of 2025-10-11 (includes 7 Trachydactylus/Bunopus records), one alignment per locus. The 12S, 16S and CYTB alignments are about 20,500 columns wide because two complete mitogenomes (AB738944, AB661661) are in every query result; trim before any tree. CMOS and MC1R alignments were re-run on 2026-10-04 and reproduce byte for byte. No tree or other analysis exists in the folder or the shell history. Reference paper: Machado et al. 2018.

Commands

esearch -db nucleotide -query "Tropiocolotes AND 12S" | efetch -format fasta > tropiocolotes_12s.fasta   # same for 16S, CMOS, MC1R, CYTB, MOS
cat tropiocolotes_12s.fasta 12S.fasta > 12s.all.fas   # likewise X.fas = GenBank first, supplied last (MOS goes into CMOS.fas)
maft X.fas   # ~/bin/maft: mafft --localpair --maxiterate 1000 --ep 0.123 --nuc --reorder --preservecase --quiet
maftm 12s.all.fas   # ~/bin/maftm: same with --adjustdirection (no --nuc), then readseq to .msf
