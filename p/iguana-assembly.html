About

Reads SRR5508305 are Brachylophus fasciatus WGS (HiSeq 2500, 31 Gb, Univ. of Lausanne). Assembly 2026-01-16, Pilon polish of scaffolds >=10 kb 2026-01-18, SINEderella scan 2026-02 with Eryx/Fiji SINE queries: Sq3X_Eryx 30,696, Sq3A_Eryx 12,382, sq2_Eryx 6,275, Heno 989 assigned copies. scaffolds500.fasta is the genome input of the SQ2/SINEdb multi-species project (symlinked from Genomes/.../Fiji, a copy is on Monsoon). An older Squam1 search in Iguana delicatissima was deleted on request. Do not move the SINEs directory.

Commands
