About

Three pieces of work share one KIT folder: a 2024-01 Squam1 SINE search in the public I. delicatissima genome; a 2026-01 assembly from reads SRR5508305 (script comment says Brachylophus fasciatus, not checked against SRA), and a 2026-02 SINEderella scan of its scaffolds with Eryx and Fiji SINE queries. No written conclusion was found. About 160 of 224 GB are regenerable intermediates.
