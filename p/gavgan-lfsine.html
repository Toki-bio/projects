Crocodilian SINEs (GavGan)

Repbase crocodilian SINEs and three LF-SINE queries searched in the gharial and the American alligator genomes; crocodilian arm of the LF-SINE-in-birds work.

parked updated 2024-07-19

About

2024-01: 25 Repbase crocodilian SINE consensi searched in Gavialis gangeticus (rGavGan2 hap2) and clustered; a RepeatModeler attempt failed immediately (missing RepeatMasker module) and left 2.3 GB of leftovers. 2024-07: lfsine.q, LFSINE-rb.q and lfsine_mid.q searched in the gharial and Alligator mississippiensis (rAllMis1): 1,657 / 979 / 308 hits in the gharial, 1,788 / 1,032 / 315 in the alligator; hits clustered into 34 groups. Same queries were used on emu, kiwi and other birds the same days. No written conclusion.

Commands

sear2kloop LFSINE-rb.q GCA_030020295.1_rGavGan2.hap2_genomic.fna   # also lfsine.q, lfsine_mid.q; same for GCF_030867095.1_rAllMis1_genomic.fna
SubFam GCA_030020295.1_rGavGan2.hap2_genomic-lfsine.bnk ; maft ...clw   # cluster hits into groups
seqkit grep -n -r -p "Croc|Allig" RepBase.bnk > croc ; seqkit grep -n -r -p SINE croc > crocSINE.repbase
